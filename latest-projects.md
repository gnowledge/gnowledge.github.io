A curated, regularly updated list of currently active and recent initiatives. Each entry links to a fuller description.

## CUBE Home Labs

A pandemic-era extension of the [CUBE program](/projects/cube.html) in which undergraduate biology investigations migrated into students' homes, sustained through [chatShaala](/projects/chatshaala-cube.html) conversations. The home-lab format has become a durable mode of project-based undergraduate research using simple model systems (snails, *Drosophila*, daphnia). See [Undergraduates in Lockdown](/publications.html) and follow-up CUBE writing for the published account.

## Living Academy

A studio for home-schooling parents and children, and a club-house for children in an urban neighbourhood near Bhavani Peth, Pune. It develops as a "life-long kindergarten" experiment in studio-based, project-led learning. Follow the work in progress at the [Making of Living Academy](https://metaStudio.org/c/la).

## nodeBook

An online deployment of [hedgedoc-nb](https://github.com/gnowgi/hedgedoc-nb) — a fork of [HedgeDoc](https://hedgedoc.org/) extended to **build knowledge graphs in markdown**. Try it at [nodebook.co.in](https://nodebook.co.in); read more on the [nodeBook project page](/projects/nodebook.html).

## naYana

A phonetic alphabet and OTF font designed for transcriptional unity across the world's writing systems, with a browser plugin that renders web pages into naYana. See the [naYana project page](/projects/naYana.html).

**New (2026):** [*Returning Time to the Reader*](/nayana/returning-time-to-the-reader.html) — an essay on naYana and the cost of English orthography.

## Roots of STEM

An ongoing series of essays at [gnowgi.substack.com](https://gnowgi.substack.com) on the cognitive, cultural and pedagogical foundations of STEM education — connecting embodied cognition, free-software practice, and project-based learning to the question of how scientific habits develop.

## Trust Conditions

A philosophy-of-science thread on **objectivity, calibration, and trust** in scientific instruments, media, and institutions. The position — *calibrational realism* — is laid out in the 2026 preprint [From Truth to Trust: Negotiating Objectivity through Calibrated Instruments, Media, and Institutions](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19420098) by [Nagarjuna G.](/team/nagarjuna-g.html).

## Sensation Modulating Network

A series of research articles and an in-progress monograph on cognitive science, developing a layered **Sensory-Motor-Network (SMN)** account in which dexterous *Halted-Action-Patterns* (HAPs) ground the emergence of syntax, symbolic operation, and conscious action. See the [Roots of beingHuman](/projects/research.html#the-roots-of-beinghuman) research thread for the underlying framework.
