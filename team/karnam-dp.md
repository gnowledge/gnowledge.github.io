## About

Durgaprasad Karnam is a member of the [Gnowledge Lab](/index.html) and a research scholar at the [Homi Bhabha Centre for Science Education](https://www.hbcse.tifr.res.in/), TIFR, working within its [Learning Sciences Research Group](https://lsr.hbcse.tifr.res.in/people/durgaprasad-karnam/). He holds a BTech (Hons) in Electrical Engineering (2011) from IIT Kharagpur, and came to education research through a [Teach For India](https://www.teachforindia.org/) fellowship and community projects with school children in Govandi and Dharavi.

His doctoral work designed **interactive new media for learning vectors** — a *Touchy-Feely Vectors* interface that supplies the geometric manipulations a paper medium cannot, used to study how students' model-based reasoning and conceptual behaviour change. The thread runs through his earlier *Interactive Vectors for Model-based Reasoning* (ICCE, 2016, with Harshit Agrawal, Deepa Mishra and Sanjay Chandrasekharan).

With [Nagarjuna G.](/team/nagarjuna-g.html), Ravi Sinha and Ashish Pardeshi, he asked how making and tinkering reshape teachers' sense of STEM in *Figuring the 'making' with Indian teachers: Can we re-imagine STEM learning?* (ICLS, 2020).

Most recently he co-authors, with [Nagarjuna G.](/team/nagarjuna-g.html), the 2026 preprint [*The Sensation Modulating Network: Haltability as the Architectural Ground for Object-Directed Phenomenology*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26856) — part of the lab's [Sensation Modulating Network](/latest-projects.html) work and the [Roots of beingHuman](/projects/research.html#the-roots-of-beinghuman) research thread, with companion code in [SMN-Hydrogen](https://github.com/gnowgi/SMN-Hydrogen).

Outside research he is an amateur flautist, and sketches and writes poetry.

## Research interests

- Cognition and embodied cognitive science
- Learning sciences — interventions for abstract concepts in science and mathematics
- Interactive new media and model-based reasoning
- Maker education and STEM learning
- Philosophy of science
