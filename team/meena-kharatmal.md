## About

Meena Kharatmal is a long-standing member of the [Gnowledge Lab](/index.html) and a researcher at the [Homi Bhabha Centre for Science Education (HBCSE), TIFR](https://www.hbcse.tifr.res.in), Mumbai. Her work sits at the intersection of biology education research, concept-based knowledge representation, and public communication of science.

Over nearly two decades, her research with the lab has focused on **how scientific knowledge is structured, taught, and learned** — particularly in biology. With [Nagarjuna G.](/team/nagarjuna-g.html), she developed *Refined Concept Mapping* (RCM), a methodology that adds rigour to traditional concept maps by constraining the predicate space, with applications in classroom teaching, knowledge assessment, and the analysis of biology textbooks.

She is a core contributor to the [CUBE program](/projects/cube.html) — Collaboratively Understanding Biology Education — and to its outreach. She regularly writes for teacher-facing magazines (*Teacher Plus*, *iwonder*, *Shaikshanik Sandarbh*, *Jeevan Shikshan*) in English, Hindi, and Marathi, bringing project-based biology investigations into mainstream pedagogy. During the COVID-19 pandemic she contributed extensively to the **CovidGyan** initiative at TIFR, producing infographics and explainers on tests, vaccines, immunization and seroprevalence studies.

## Research interests

- Concept mapping methodology and the analysis of scientific knowledge structures
- Undergraduate biology education — experimental design, scientific method, conceptual understanding
- Evidence-based teaching practices (concept inventories, diagnostic questions)
- Project-based and collaborative biology learning (CUBE)
- Public communication of science and microbiology
- Multilingual science writing for teachers and students

## Projects

- [CUBE: Collaboratively Understanding Biology Education](/projects/cube.html) — investigative undergraduate biology research using simple model systems
- [Concept Mapping research](/projects/conceptmapping.html) — long-running development of Refined Concept Mapping (RCM) with Nagarjuna G.
- [Biology Education Research](/projects/stemedu.html#biology-education-research) — lead on multiple ongoing studies: undergraduate students' understanding of experimental design; scientific method through research reading; natural selection concept inventories; evidence-based teaching at undergraduate level
- **Vigyan Pratibha** — co-authored the *Microorganisms at our Doorstep* learning unit with Nagarjuna G. and M. C. Arunan
- **CovidGyan (TIFR)** — infographics and explainers during 2020–2021 on COVID-19 testing, vaccines, immunization, and seroprevalence
- [chatShaala](/projects/chatshaalaApp.html) and [STEM Games / metaStudio](/projects/stemgames.html) — community platforms for the lab's projects
