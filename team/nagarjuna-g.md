## About

Nagarjuna G. is the principal investigator of the [Gnowledge Lab](/index.html). He is a Former Professor at the [Homi Bhabha Centre for Science Education (HBCSE), TIFR](https://www.hbcse.tifr.res.in), Mumbai, and is currently a Visiting Faculty at IISER Pune.

His work sits at the intersection of cognitive science, science education, semantic computing, and the free software / knowledge commons movement. He founded the Gnowledge Lab as a space for **designing and developing discourse-based, collaborative, constructionist learning environments** — what the lab calls *studios* — and for building the open-source infrastructure that supports them. The unusual spelling *gnowledge* is an homage to the [GNU project](https://www.gnu.org/), reflecting the lab's commitment to free software and openly licensed knowledge.

Over two decades, his research has explored the biological and cognitive roots of knowledge, the structure and growth of concepts, and how technology can make learning *visible* and *participatory*. His longest-running technical project, [GNOWSYS](/projects/gnowsys.html), is a graph-based framework for semantic computing that powers several of the lab's platforms.

## Research interests

- Constructionist and connectionist models of STEM education
- Semantic computing and graph-based knowledge representation (GNOWSYS / gstudio)
- Concept mapping methodology and the structure of scientific knowledge
- Biological and cognitive roots of knowledge; perception, conception, and conscious action
- Knowledge networks as complex systems
- Free software, copyleft, and the politics of knowledge commons
- Writing systems and phonetic alphabets (naYana)

## Software

Code repositories maintained by Nagarjuna G.:

- [**hedgedoc-nb**](https://github.com/gnowgi/hedgedoc-nb) — *"Build knowledge graphs in markdown."* A TypeScript fork of [HedgeDoc](https://hedgedoc.org/) extending its real-time collaborative markdown editor with knowledge-graph notebook features.
- [**naYana-tutor**](https://github.com/gnowledge/naYana-tutor) — source code of the [naYana tutor web app](https://nayana.gnowledge.org) (*Learn, Type, Read* IPA through naYana, with in-browser audio playback via [Piper](https://github.com/rhasspy/piper)).
- [**SMN-Hydrogen**](https://github.com/gnowgi/SMN-Hydrogen) — *"The 'Hydrogen atom' of the SMN — the smallest code that exhibits the eight architectural registers."* Reference Python simulations for the **Sensation Modulating Network** architecture; companion code to a forthcoming paper with Durgaprasad Karnam, and to the [Roots of beingHuman](/projects/research.html#the-roots-of-beinghuman) research thread.
