## About

Prof. M. C. Arunan is a long-time member of the [Gnowledge Lab](/index.html) and the originating spirit behind the [CUBE program](/projects/cube.html) — Collaboratively Understanding Biology Education — one of the lab's flagship initiatives in undergraduate science education.

His work has been driven by a question that animates much of the lab's pedagogy: *can a small undergraduate research lab, equipped with frugal model systems, sustain frontline biology questions?* The CUBE program — running since 2012 across colleges in many Indian cities — answers that question by introducing simple model organisms (daphnia, snails, *Drosophila*) into student-led, investigative research projects. He has been a sustained advocate for shifting undergraduate biology from textbook recitation to *doing* science, framing this in his metaphor of "**populating India with STEM spectators**" — building the audience for science the way street cricket builds an audience for the sport.

He has co-authored, with [Meena Kharatmal](/team/meena-kharatmal.html) and [Nagarjuna G.](/team/nagarjuna-g.html), several learning units and research papers on biology education — most notably the *Microorganisms at our Doorstep* unit (Vigyan Pratibha, HBCSE, 2019/2021) and the natural-selection concept inventory study.

## Research interests

- Undergraduate biology education through investigative research
- Simple model systems (daphnia, snails, *Drosophila*) as research platforms in resource-constrained labs
- Conceptual understanding of evolution and natural selection
- University–institute collaboration models for science education
- STEM culture and citizen-science framing of undergraduate research
